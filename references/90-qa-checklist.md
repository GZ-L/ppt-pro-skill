# 90 Presentation QA Checklist

Use this before claiming a deck package is ready.

## Story

- The deck has one clear thesis.
- The opening explains why the audience should care.
- Every slide supports the thesis.
- Page order creates a visible argument or journey.
- Section breaks improve pacing instead of adding filler.
- The close gives a takeaway, decision, next action, or memorable conclusion.

## Slide Density

- Each slide has one main message.
- Titles say the point, not just the topic.
- Body text is short enough to scan.
- Speaker notes hold details that do not belong on the slide.
- No page is used as a document dump.

## Visual Hierarchy

- The first-view focus is obvious.
- Font sizes and weights are consistent by role.
- Margins and alignment follow a clear grid.
- Whitespace is intentional.
- Color emphasis is reserved for important information.
- Footer, page number, and source notes do not compete with the main content.

## Aesthetic Quality

- Cover, section pages, body pages, and closing page feel like one system.
- Palette is not random or one-note.
- Images are high quality, correctly cropped, and not stretched.
- Icons and diagrams clarify instead of decorating.
- Charts and tables are readable and explain a point.
- Screenshots are cropped, annotated, and legible.

## PPTX Editability

- Text is editable where possible.
- Shapes, charts, and tables are not flattened unnecessarily.
- Slide masters, repeated headers, and footers are consistent when applicable.
- Images keep aspect ratio.
- The file opens in the target software.
- Optional PDF export matches the PPTX visually.

## Visual Web Deck

- Preview opens successfully.
- Desktop and mobile framing are acceptable if responsive use matters.
- Text does not overlap or overflow.
- Motion helps pacing or comprehension.
- Assets load locally or through approved public URLs.
- Export path is documented if images, PDF, or video are needed.

## Confirmation And Director Control

- User confirmation was recorded before final production, unless the user explicitly asked to skip questions.
- Page count, final deliverable, style direction, and rewrite boundary are confirmed.
- No second PPT Skill was used as a separate director.
- Any local renderer or code library was treated as mechanical production only.

## Accessibility And Delivery

- Body text and chart labels have enough contrast.
- Important content is not conveyed by color alone.
- External sources, quotes, screenshots, and data are attributed.
- Final filenames are clear.
- Known limitations are listed.
- Next revision suggestions are concrete.
